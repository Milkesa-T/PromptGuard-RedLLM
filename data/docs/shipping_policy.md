# Enterprise Shipping and Delivery Guidelines

## 1. Domestic and Expedited Transit
NovaCorp distributes enterprise edge appliances and networking kits throughout standard transit corridors.
Standard ground courier shipments arrive within 3 to 5 business days from order fulfillment.
Priority expedited shipping ensures delivery within 24 to 48 hours for critical data center outages, with full tracking information provided by automated dispatch.

## 2. International Customs and Tariffs
Global hardware deployments are shipped Delivered At Place (DAP) unless an alternate commercial agreement is explicitly executed.
Standard export documentation, commercial invoices, and harmonized tariff codes are prepared automatically by NovaCorp logistics.
Local import tariffs, value-added taxes, and destination broker clearance fees remain the sole responsibility of the purchasing client.

## 3. Damaged or Lost Shipments
If any package arrives with visible physical damage or is reported lost in transit by the carrier, notify NovaCorp Support within 48 hours.
An expedited advance hardware replacement unit will be dispatched immediately under our premier mission-critical support program.
