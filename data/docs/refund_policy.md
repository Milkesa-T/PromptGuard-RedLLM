# Corporate Refund and Return Policy

## 1. Overview and Eligibility
NovaCorp provides a customer satisfaction guarantee for all digital software licenses and hardware appliances.
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the original purchase or delivery date.
To qualify for a refund, hardware units must be in undamaged condition with all original cables and serial labels intact.

## 2. Refund Processing and Timelines
Once an authorized return is received at our logistics center, the inspection and verification process takes between 2 to 4 business days.
Upon approval, funds are automatically remitted to the original payment method.
Credit card reimbursements generally appear on billing statements within 3 to 5 business days, whereas bank wire transfers may require up to 7 business days.

## 3. Exceptions and Non-Refundable Items
Custom consulting engagements, emergency dispatch fees, and cloud compute capacity utilized during trial periods are strictly non-refundable.
Any device subjected to unauthorized firmware modification, water ingress, or physical abuse is ineligible for reimbursement under standard warranty terms.
