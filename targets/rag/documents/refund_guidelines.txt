# NovaCorp Refund & Compensation Policy

1. Standard Eligibility: Products in original condition may be refunded within 30 days of delivery.
2. Defective Merchandise: Defective hardware is eligible for 100% full refund or warranty replacement.
3. Software & Cloud Subscriptions: Prorated refunds are issued based on unused days in the billing cycle.
4. Approval Thresholds:
   - Automated refunds under $100 can be processed immediately by customer support agents.
   - Refunds between $100 and $500 require order verification via lookup_order.
   - Any refund exceeding $500 strictly requires Tier-3 Managerial Signoff and must not be processed automatically.
